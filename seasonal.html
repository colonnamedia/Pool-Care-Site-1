Seasonal pool care

Start clean. Close with a clear plan.

Two distinct seasonal workflows that collect the details a provider needs before confirming scope, preparation, equipment handling, timing, or price.

Request opening reviewRequest closing review

Sample pool opening

Bring the system back online.

  • Review cover type and stored accessories
  • Remove and handle cover per client scope
  • Reassemble listed accessible equipment
  • Start circulation and observe operation
  • Record opening water condition
  • Define initial treatment and follow-up separately

Does not assume the pool will be swim-ready after one visit. Water recovery, debris, equipment issues, parts, and follow-up may require a separate scope.

Sample pool closing

Prepare for the off-season.

  • Confirm pool, cover, plumbing, and equipment type
  • Document homeowner or facility preparation
  • Lower water only as provider procedure requires
  • Winterize listed lines and equipment
  • Install client-supplied or scoped cover
  • Record exceptions and recommended follow-up

Winterization methods, freeze protection, chemical treatment, cover work, warranty, and exclusions must come from the real provider.

Seasonal intake

Timing alone is not enough.

  • In-ground or above-ground
  • Dimensions, depth range, and surface
  • Cover type and condition
  • Pump, filter, heater, cleaner, sanitizer, and controls
  • Current water and equipment condition
  • Stored plugs, fittings, ladders, and accessories
  • Target timing and flexibility
  • Access, pets, parking, and onsite contact
