# FireWorks Project — Clearline Pool Care

## Template family and demo safety

Recurring Property Services. Primary demo industry: pool maintenance. Clearline Pool Care is fictional. All names, phone numbers, emails, reviews, credentials, licenses, certifications, hours, coverage, availability, response times, services, treatment standards, prices, plan terms, chemical allowances, and claims are sample content. The quote form validates locally only. It does not send or store inquiries, upload documents, reserve or confirm appointments, create recurring schedules, enroll customers, save payment methods, or take payments.

## SEO, schema, and design

Every page contains an editable title, description, canonical URL, semantic headings, internal links, responsive navigation, robots file, and XML sitemap. Home includes clearly fictional LocalBusiness schema. The coverage hub avoids thin city duplicates, fabricated routes, and ranking promises. Add location pages only when distinct verified content exists. The original hero is stored as an optimized 1920×1080 WebP (approximately 116 KB); the storefront preview is 1200×675 WebP (approximately 63 KB). Both are composed for left-side headline legibility and a mobile-safe technician crop.

Image generation prompt summary: photorealistic commercial editorial scene of a credible navy-shirt pool technician testing water beside a clean residential in-ground pool, technician on the right, uncluttered darker pool space on the left, natural late-morning light, no logos, text, claims, extra workers, or unsafe behavior. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then optimized locally to WebP.
