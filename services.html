Routine maintenance

A visit customers can understand.

This checklist is illustrative. A real client supplies exact tasks, treatment standards, weather rules, chemical policy, timing, and visit documentation.

W

Water & chemistry

  • Record sample test readings
  • Apply client-approved treatment scope
  • Note unusual clarity or algae conditions
  • Explain included chemical limits
S

Surface & circulation

  • Skim accessible surface debris
  • Check baskets and visible returns
  • Brush listed walls and steps
  • Vacuum according to plan scope
E

Equipment observation

  • Observe pump and filter operation
  • Note visible leaks or unusual noise
  • Record pressure when applicable
  • Escalate concerns for approval

Condition-based requests

Some pools need a different starting point.

Pool condition changes the work. The site invites honest intake without guaranteeing a treatment result, price, or timeline.

Clear, currently maintained

Routine plan evaluation with pool size, equipment, current provider status, access, and preferred frequency.

Cloudy or showing algae

Condition review, recent treatment notes, equipment operation, and provider assessment before scope.

Green, neglected, or newly acquired

Separate recovery review may be needed. No standard visit or guaranteed turnaround is assumed.
